## 3. Video 1 production brief

**Title:** *Build a Free Expense Tracker That Catches Its Own Mistakes* (58 characters; passes the Phase 1 checks)
- Alt A: *This Expense Tracker Refuses Bad Entries (Free Google Sheets Template)*. Over 60 characters, so only if you set the title rule to warn.
- Alt B: *Stop Typos From Wrecking Your Expense Totals (Free Template)*

**Thumbnail:** `Video1_thumbnail_concept.jpg`: a spreadsheet with one red ✗ row and the words **IT CAUGHT IT**. It doesn't repeat any title word.

**Hook, the first 15 seconds** (advisory score 55, "workable"):
> If your expense tracker accepts a typo, your totals are wrong and you will not know why. Here is a free tracker that catches its own mistakes before they cost you.

*On screen:* a total jumps when a "$48" typed as text quietly disappears from the sum, then the tracker flags it in red.

**Promise:** by the end, the viewer has their own copy of the template, understands each of the six rules, and knows a 2-minute weekly routine.

**Target length:** 8–10 minutes, about 1,200–1,500 narrated words. That fits the engine's existing 1,200–1,800-word script gate.

### Outline (chapters)
| # | Chapter | What happens on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The typo your total never told you about | Wrong total vs the flagged row (hook). |
| 1 | Make your own copy and set three rules | Settings tab: tax year, receipt rule, review rule. Each is labelled "your own rule, not a legal threshold". |
| 2 | Rule 1: dates you can trust | Type 2025-12-30 → "Date outside tax year". Type text → "not a real date". |
| 3 | Rule 2: amounts must be real numbers | -89.99 → ERROR. Explain that refunds go in Notes. |
| 4 | Rule 3: why the dropdown isn't enough | Paste "Meal" past the dropdown → the rule column still catches it. Bridges to the One-Cent idea. |
| 5 | Rule 4: catch duplicates | Same date, vendor and amount → both rows CHECK. COUNTIFS shown in plain words. |
| 6 | Rules 5–6: receipts and large amounts | Your policy flags; it never blocks. |
| 7 | ERROR vs CHECK: what counts in your totals | ERROR rows are left out, and the excluded amount is shown. |
| 8 | The Summary that checks itself | Category and monthly totals must add up to the total; status turns to ALL CHECKS PASSED. |
| 9 | Your 2-minute weekly routine | Enter → fix ERRORs → review CHECKs → confirm the green status. |
| 10 | Next: test your rules at the edge | End screen to the One-Cent video. |
